About the author

Abha Mehta

Mrs. Abha Mehta is a French linguist with over 36 years of experience of teaching French language. She teaches at leading Management Institutes and has authored ten books and YouTube audios to help beginners of French language. The President of India Shri. K.R. Narayanan released her books at Rashtrapati Bhawan.

“A child doesn't fall in love with grammar. They fall in love with a word, a song, a story — grammar can wait.”

Honourable President of India Sh. K.R. Narayanan releasing Je Parle Français series of books in a function held in Ashoka Hall, Rashtrapati Bhawan, New Delhi on July 2, 1998.
